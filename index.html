About Me

I am a pre-final year Computer Science Engineering undergraduate at KLE Technological University. I am a full-stack and AI-focused software engineer with a singular focus: building LLM products and production web applications that can truly be trusted.

Rather than treating generative models as unpredictable black boxes prone to inventing figures, I architect systems with strict boundaries — isolating LLMs strictly to language comprehension while keeping financial and business calculations 100% deterministic with strict anti-hallucination refusals. This methodology led our rural financial advisory platform to win 1st Runner-Up among 50 teams at Build for Billions (NITK).

My engineering stack spans Python, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Docker, REST APIs, and RAG (Qdrant). Whether deploying containerized role-based portals on Google Cloud Run, engineering real-time WebSocket AI interview platforms on Vercel and Railway, or writing automated tests (215+ tests shipped across systems), I design for verifiable correctness, robust access control, and high performance.
